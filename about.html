✦ Our Story

About Alabaster Health & Aesthetics

A South African luxury medical aesthetics brand built on professional training, advanced technology, and personalised care — where beauty, health, and wellness come together.

✦ Meet The Founder

Founded By Murendeni Mudau

Alabaster Health & Aesthetics was founded by Murendeni Mudau, a qualified Somatologist and Beauty Therapist with over 7 years of hands-on experience in the beauty, skin, and aesthetics industry.

What began as a passion for helping clients feel confident in their own skin has grown into a multi-branch clinic and accredited training college — serving clients and students across Gauteng and Limpopo.

Under Murendeni's leadership, Alabaster has become a trusted name for advanced non-invasive treatments, authentic Korean Lemon Bottle lipolysis, and industry-accredited aesthetic training.

✦ What Drives Us

Our Mission, Vision & Core Values

Every treatment, every class, and every client interaction is guided by these pillars.

Our Mission

To provide professional, personalised, and results-driven aesthetic treatments in a safe, welcoming environment — while empowering the next generation of beauty professionals through accredited training.

Our Vision

To become South Africa's most trusted luxury medical aesthetics brand — known for clinical excellence, authentic products, and compassionate care across every branch.

Our Values

Integrity, professionalism, client safety, and continuous education. We never compromise on the quality of products, machines, or the care we provide.

Safety First

All treatments are performed using sterile, certified equipment and authentic products. Every client is assessed before any procedure.

Personalised Care

No two clients are the same. Every treatment plan is customised to your unique skin, body, and wellness goals — from consultation to aftercare.

Proven Results

We measure success by visible client transformations and long-term satisfaction — not just sales. Our reviews speak for themselves.
